A top-down stealth heist game: ghost the guard cones, crack the Meridian Vault, grab the Aurum Reserve and get out before the alarm meter fills. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best heist game" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Play

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network, vanilla JS.

## How to play

- **WASD / arrows** — move. Hold **Shift** to sneak (slower, quieter, harder to spot).
- **Q or click** — toss a coin to distract a guard (limited coins).
- **E** — EMP pulse: disables cameras for 6 s, costs battery, spikes the alarm a little.
- **Hold F** — lift loot, crack the vault, pick a lock, hack a camera console, pickpocket a keycard.
- **M** — mute.

Three rooms, one night: the Lobby, the Counting House, the Meridian Vault. Grab the loot, reach the extraction point, and don't let the alarm meter fill.
